Reporting a correction

If an error-code meaning on this site is wrong for your model, include: the brand, the full model number, the exact code as displayed, and what your model's manual says it means. That is enough for us to verify and correct the page.

What we cannot help with

We cannot diagnose an individual appliance by message, recommend a specific repairer, or quote repair prices. For a fault that needs hands-on work, see when to call a qualified technician.

About this site

An independent, free guide to household appliance error codes. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any appliance manufacturer. Meanings vary by model — always confirm in your exact model's manual.
