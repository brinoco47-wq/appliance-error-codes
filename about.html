About Us

Appliance Error Codes & Troubleshooting is an independent, free information site. It exists for one job: help you understand the code on your appliance's display, work out the likely cause, and decide what to do next.

How our content is written

Error-code meanings on this site are cross-checked against multiple published sources, including manufacturer user manuals and manufacturer support pages where available. Where sources disagree, or where a meaning depends on the exact model, we say so rather than pick a confident-sounding answer. We never invent manufacturer-specific meanings, model compatibility, parts, prices, or official citations.

What this site is not

  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any appliance manufacturer.
  • We are not a repair service and we do not take bookings.
  • We do not claim to cover every manufacturer or every error code. Categories and codes are added as they are verified.
  • Nothing here replaces your model's official manual or a qualified technician's judgment.

Corrections

If you spot a wrong meaning, tell us via the contact page with the brand, model, and code. Corrections are made as soon as they are verified.
