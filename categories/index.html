Appliances

Browse error codes by appliance type. Only verified entries are published — categories without entries yet link to the general guides instead of guessed codes.

Washing Machines

Front-load and top-load washers. The most common codes are water, drain, balance, and door faults — most have a simple first check.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Fridges, freezers, and ice makers. Verified error-code entries for this category are being added — start with the guides below.

Dishwashers

Built-in and portable dishwashers. Drain, leak, and fill faults dominate — most are a filter, a hose, or a float switch.

Ovens & Ranges

Wall ovens, ranges, and cooktops. Verified error-code entries for this category are being added — start with the guides below.

Microwaves

Countertop and over-range microwaves. Verified error-code entries for this category are being added — start with the guides below.

Clothes Dryers

Electric and gas dryers. Airflow warnings (Flow Sense and friends) are the most urgent codes a dryer can show — they are fire-safety alerts.

Air Conditioners

Split, window, and portable air conditioners. Verified error-code entries for this category are being added — start with the guides below.

Water Heaters

Tank, tankless, and heat-pump water heaters. Verified error-code entries for this category are being added — start with the guides below.

About this site
