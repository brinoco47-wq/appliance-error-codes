How to Find Your Appliance Model Number

Guide · 2026-10-19

Nearly every error-code answer depends on the model number. Manufacturers reuse codes across model families, so the model number is what turns a generic page into the right answer. It is printed on a rating plate (also called a serial plate) somewhere on the machine.

Where to look, by appliance

  • Washing machine: inside the door frame, on the back panel, or behind the detergent drawer.
  • Dryer: inside the door opening, or on the back panel.
  • Refrigerator: on an inside wall of the fridge or freezer compartment, or on the side of the door.
  • Dishwasher: on the edge of the door, or on the side wall of the tub (visible when the door is open).
  • Oven / range: on the door frame, behind the storage drawer, or on the back.
  • Water heater: on a label on the side of the tank, or the front of a tankless unit.
  • Air conditioner: on a side or rear panel label.

What to write down

Copy the full model number and the serial number, exactly as printed — including any suffix letters. A photo of the plate is even better: support lines and parts lookups use the exact string. If the plate is worn, a photo taken at an angle with a flashlight often reveals it.

If you cannot find the plate

Check the original manual, the purchase receipt, or the manufacturer's app if the appliance was registered. As a last resort, the brand's support line can sometimes identify a model from a photo of the control panel — but a rating-plate photo is always faster.
