Privacy Policy

This site is a static information website. It has no accounts, no login, and no comment system.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Reading a page, using the on-site search, and filtering lists all happen in your browser — search terms are not sent to us or to any server.

Server logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as the page requested and the time) for security and reliability. We do not control those logs and we do not use them to identify visitors.

Advertising

No advertising code is currently loaded on this site. If advertising is added in future, this policy will be updated to name the network and what it collects, before any ad code goes live.

External links

Links to manufacturer manuals and support pages leave this site. Their privacy practices are their own — check their policies before sharing personal details with them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be noted here with a new date.
