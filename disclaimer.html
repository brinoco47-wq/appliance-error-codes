Disclaimer

Safety notice: Troubleshooting information on this site is general guidance only. Error-code meanings vary by brand and model, and a wrong guess on a gas, water, or high-voltage appliance can cause injury or property damage. Always consult your exact model's official manual first.

What this site does not do

  • It does not diagnose your individual appliance.
  • It does not replace a manufacturer's manual, a warranty service, or a qualified technician.
  • It does not cover every manufacturer, model, or code — absence of a code here does not mean the code is unimportant.

Do not do these things

  • Do not open an energized appliance or work on live electrical parts.
  • Do not bypass safety systems, door locks, thermal cut-outs, or float switches.
  • Do not handle gas valves, gas connectors, or sealed refrigeration systems without appropriate qualifications.
  • Do not run a machine that smells of burning, leaks water onto electrics, or shows a severe airflow warning.

If you smell gas

Do not touch electrical switches or phones inside the building. Leave immediately and call your gas utility or emergency services from outside.

When in doubt, stop

If a step feels unsafe, is not in your model's manual, or needs a tool you do not understand, stop and call a qualified technician. See when to call a qualified technician.

About this site

An independent, free guide to household appliance error codes. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any appliance manufacturer. Meanings vary by model — always confirm in your exact model's manual.
