Samsung dE — Door error — the door is not firmly closed

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerSamsung
Error codedE Also shown as: DE.
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The door is not firmly closed, or laundry is caught in it, so the door lock cannot engage. The washer will not run until the door is properly shut.

Common possible causes

  • Laundry caught in the door or seal
  • The door not pushed fully shut
  • A faulty door latch (less common)
Advertisement

Safe initial checks

  • Look around the door and seal for trapped clothing
  • Push the door firmly and listen for the latch
  • Check the latch area for damage

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Pause or switch off the machine.
  2. Open the door and remove anything caught in it.
  3. Check the seal sits flat, then close the door firmly.
  4. Restart the cycle.

When a reset may help

A power cycle does not fix a door error — the latch must engage. If it cannot, the latch needs attention.

When to call a professional

  • The latch is broken or the door will not stay shut
  • The code persists with a clean seal and a firmly closed door
Safety warning: Never force the door while the drum is moving, and never defeat the door lock. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Is Samsung dE the same as LG dE?

The meaning is the same — a door that is not closed or latched — but always follow your own brand's manual for the exact checks.
