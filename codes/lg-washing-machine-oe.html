LG OE — Drain error — the washer is not draining

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerLG
Error codeOE
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

Water is not leaving the drum within the time the washer expects. The machine usually stops with water still sitting in the drum, and the spin may not run.

Common possible causes

  • A kinked or blocked drain hose
  • A clogged drain pump filter (lint, coins, small objects)
  • A drain hose positioned too high, or its end submerged in standing water
  • Too much detergent creating excess suds that slow draining
  • A faulty drain pump

Safe initial checks

  • Look along the drain hose for kinks or crushing
  • Check the pump filter at the bottom front of the machine for debris
  • Check the drain hose height and that its end is not sitting in water
  • Look for excessive foam in the drum

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Switch off and unplug the machine. Keep towels and a shallow tray ready — water will come out of the filter.
  2. Open the pump filter cover, drain the remaining water slowly, then remove and clean the filter.
  3. Check the drain hose along its full length for kinks, and confirm it is routed correctly with no sagging loops full of water.
  4. If there are heavy suds, run a rinse-only cycle with no detergent to clear them.
  5. Refit the filter firmly, plug in, and run a drain or spin cycle.
  6. If the pump hums but nothing drains, or the code returns with a clean filter and a clear hose, the pump is the likely fault.

When a reset may help

A power cycle clears a one-off glitch, but it cannot clear a blocked filter or hose. If OE returns on the next cycle, the blockage or pump fault is still there.

When to call a professional

  • The code persists with a clean filter and a clear hose
  • The drain pump makes no sound at all, or hums loudly without pumping
  • Water leaks from the pump area
  • You cannot reach or open the pump filter
Safety warning: Unplug before opening the pump filter. Expect a litre or more of water — work slowly and keep the area dry before restoring power. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open the door when OE is showing?

Not while water is in the drum — front-load doors stay locked for safety. Drain through the pump filter first, then the door should release.

Does too much detergent really cause OE?

Yes, indirectly. Excess suds slow the pump and can trip the drain timeout, especially with non-HE detergent in an HE machine.
