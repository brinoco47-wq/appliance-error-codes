LG d80 — Flow Sense warning — the exhaust vent is about 80% blocked

ApplianceClothes Dryers
ManufacturerLG
Error coded80 Also shown as: D80.
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The dryer's Flow Sense system has detected that the exhaust duct is about 80% blocked. The dryer still runs, but drying takes longer and the machine runs hotter. d75 means 75% blocked, d90 means 90%, and d95 means 95% — the higher the number, the more urgent the blockage. These are safety warnings, not dryer malfunctions.

Common possible causes

  • Lint buildup in the exhaust duct
  • A crushed, kinked, or damaged exhaust hose
  • A long duct run, or one with many elbows, that traps lint
  • A blocked outside vent hood (lint, debris, or a stuck flap)
  • Foil or plastic ducting instead of rigid or semi-rigid metal

Safe initial checks

  • Clean the lint filter — wash it with soap if it feels coated, and dry it fully
  • Look at the exhaust hose for crushing or kinks
  • Check the outside vent hood: the flap should open freely when the dryer runs
  • Run a timed dry with an empty drum and feel for strong airflow outside

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Unplug the dryer. If it is a gas dryer, shut the gas off as well.
  2. Remove and clean the lint filter. If it feels coated, wash it with soap and water and dry it completely before refitting.
  3. Disconnect the exhaust hose and check it for kinks, crushing, and lint. Vacuum lint out of the hose.
  4. Brush or vacuum lint along the full vent path. If the duct is long or inaccessible, hire a vent cleaning specialist — they have the right tools.
  5. Clear the outside vent hood and confirm the flap opens freely.
  6. Reconnect everything, use rigid or semi-rigid metal duct where possible, and run a timed dry with an empty drum. Strong airflow outside means the blockage is cleared.

When a reset may help

There is no reset for Flow Sense codes — they clear when airflow is restored. If the code returns after cleaning, the blockage is deeper in the duct, or the blower fan is faulty.

When to call a professional

  • The duct is long, built in, or you cannot reach the full path
  • The code returns after a full cleaning
  • There is a burning smell — stop using the dryer immediately
  • Note: on gas dryers, a d80 code can also indicate a lack of gas supply
Safety warning: Reduced airflow is a fire risk if ignored — lint plus heat is the classic dryer fire combination. Do not keep running a dryer showing d90 or d95. On gas dryers, shut the gas off before disconnecting anything. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between d75, d80, d90, and d95?

They are the same warning at different severities: roughly 75%, 80%, 90%, and 95% exhaust blockage. d80 gives you time to clean; d90 and d95 mean stop and clean now.

How often should I clean the vent system?

Clean the lint filter after every load, and deep-clean the vent system every 6–12 months — more often with long ducts or heavy use.
