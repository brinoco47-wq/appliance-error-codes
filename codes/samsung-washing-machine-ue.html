Samsung UE — Unbalanced load — the drum cannot spin evenly

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerSamsung
Error codeUE
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The laundry load is unbalanced, so the washer cannot spin it safely. Samsung's own manual notes that washing a single item — such as a bathrobe or a pair of jeans — commonly ends with this message, and the final spin result may be unsatisfactory.

Common possible causes

  • A single heavy item washed alone
  • Laundry bunched to one side of the drum
  • Very small loads that cannot spread around the drum

Safe initial checks

  • Pause and look at how the load is sitting
  • Check whether the code follows one particular item
  • Check the machine stands level and does not rock

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Pause the cycle and wait for the door to unlock.
  2. Redistribute the load evenly around the drum.
  3. For a single heavy item, add a few towels to balance it.
  4. Close the door and restart the spin.

When a reset may help

No reset is needed. UE is the machine protecting itself from an uneven spin. Fix the load and the code clears.

When to call a professional

  • UE appears with normal, balanced, everyday loads
  • The drum bangs violently even when the load is even
  • The machine has moved across the floor
Safety warning: Wait for the drum to stop completely before opening the door. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Samsung mention bathrobes and jeans specifically?

Because a single heavy, absorbent item is the classic trigger: it soaks up water, gets very heavy, and cannot spread around the drum on its own.

Is Samsung UE the same as LG UE?

The meaning is the same — an unbalanced load — but the machines are different, so always follow the steps for your own brand and model.
