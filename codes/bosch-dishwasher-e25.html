Bosch E25 — Drain pump blocked — the pump impeller is jammed

ApplianceDishwashers
ManufacturerBosch
Error codeE25
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The drain pump is blocked or jammed — usually by debris at the pump, or because the pump cover is loose. The dishwasher cannot pump water out, so the cycle stops.

Common possible causes

  • Debris at the pump (glass shards, seeds, bones, small objects)
  • A loose or incorrectly seated white pump cover
  • A damaged impeller or a failing pump

Safe initial checks

  • Remove the sump filter and look down at the pump area
  • Check whether the white pump cover is loose or lifted
  • Look for visible debris around the impeller

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Switch off and unplug the dishwasher.
  2. Remove the filter from the bottom of the tub and scoop out standing water.
  3. Pop off the white plastic pump cover and remove any debris around the impeller. Work carefully — glass is common here; wear gloves.
  4. Reseat the pump cover firmly until it clicks.
  5. Refit the filter, restore power, and run a short cycle to test draining.

When a reset may help

A reset cannot clear a jammed impeller. The debris must come out first.

When to call a professional

  • The impeller is broken or the pump shaft wobbles
  • The pump makes no sound at all when it should drain
  • E25 returns with a clean, correctly covered pump
Safety warning: Unplug before opening the pump cover. Wear gloves — broken glass in the sump is the most common injury in this job. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between E24 and E25?

E24 is the general drain fault (hose, filter, connection). E25 points at the pump itself being blocked or jammed.

Do I need tools to open the pump cover?

Usually not — the white cover lifts off by hand on most Bosch models. Your manual shows the exact method for your model.
