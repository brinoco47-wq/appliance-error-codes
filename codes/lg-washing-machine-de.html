LG dE — Door error — the door is not closed or latched

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerLG
Error codedE Also shown as: DE, dE1, dE2.
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The door (or lid) is not fully closed, or the door lock is not engaging. The machine will not start or continue a cycle until the door is properly latched.

Common possible causes

  • Laundry caught in the door seal
  • The door not pushed fully shut
  • A misaligned door or a faulty door latch / interlock

Safe initial checks

  • Look around the rubber door seal for trapped clothing
  • Push the door firmly and listen for the latch click
  • Check the latch area for visible damage

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Pause or switch off the machine.
  2. Open the door, remove anything caught in the seal, and check the seal sits flat.
  3. Close the door firmly until it latches.
  4. Restart the cycle.
  5. If the code stays with a clean, firmly closed door, the latch or interlock is the likely fault.

When a reset may help

A power cycle does not fix a door error. The latch must engage — if it cannot, the part needs attention.

When to call a professional

  • The latch is visibly broken or the door will not stay shut
  • The code persists with a clean seal and a firmly closed door
  • The door lock makes repeated clicking sounds without locking
Safety warning: Never force the door while the drum is spinning, and never defeat the door lock — it prevents the drum from opening mid-cycle. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What do dE1 and dE2 mean?

They are sub-variants of the same door fault family: the door is open, or the lock mechanism is not engaging. The checks are the same — start with obstructions and a firm close.

Can a worn door seal cause dE?

Yes, if the seal stops the door from sitting square in the frame. A seal that is folded, torn, or swollen with detergent residue can prevent latching.
