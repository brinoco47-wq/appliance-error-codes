Bosch E15 — Leak detected — water in the base tray

ApplianceDishwashers
ManufacturerBosch
Error codeE15
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

Water has collected in the base tray underneath the dishwasher, lifting the safety float switch. The machine stops the water supply and pauses the cycle to protect your floor. E15 means there is a real leak (or a stuck float) — not a software glitch.

Common possible causes

  • A small leak from a hose, seal, or the sump area
  • A stuck or faulty float switch
  • A water inlet valve that does not shut fully
  • Over-foaming that pushes water out of the tub

Safe initial checks

  • Look under and around the machine for visible water
  • Check the inlet hose and drain hose connections for drips
  • Check the door seal for damage or trapped debris

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Turn the water supply off and unplug the dishwasher.
  2. Mop up any visible water and check hose connections and the door seal.
  3. The base tray sits under the machine: draining it (a commonly published step is to tilt the machine carefully to let the tray water out, and to dry the polystyrene float) may clear the code — but it does not fix the leak.
  4. Dry the area, restore water and power, and run a short cycle while watching for drips.
  5. If E15 returns, water is still reaching the tray. The leak source must be found and repaired.

When a reset may help

A reset or a drained tray only buys time. If the tray refills, the code will come back — the leak, valve, or float is still faulty. Do not keep resetting and running the machine with an active leak.

When to call a professional

  • E15 returns after the tray is drained and dried
  • You can see water dripping but cannot find the source
  • The float switch or inlet valve is suspected
  • The machine is built in and you cannot safely reach the base
Safety warning: Unplug before touching anything wet, and keep the water supply off until the leak is found. Never run a dishwasher with an active leak — water damage spreads fast, and water plus electrics is dangerous. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Why does E15 keep coming back after I reset it?

Because resetting does not repair the leak. The tray refills from the same drip, the float lifts again, and E15 returns. Find the drip first.

Can I just keep draining the tray?

No. Repeated tray water means an ongoing leak. Apart from floor damage, running the machine this way risks electrical damage.
