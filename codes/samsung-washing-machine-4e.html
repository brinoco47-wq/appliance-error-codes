Samsung 4E — Water supply error — the washer is not filling

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerSamsung
Error code4E Also shown as: 4C.
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The washer is not getting water, or not enough of it. Newer Samsung models show the same fault as 4C. The machine stops the wash because it cannot fill the drum.

Common possible causes

  • A water supply tap that is closed
  • Low household water pressure
  • A kinked inlet hose
  • Clogged mesh filters at the water hose connections

Safe initial checks

  • Confirm the supply tap is fully open
  • Check the water pressure at another tap
  • Look for kinks in the inlet hose
  • Check the mesh filters for debris

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Make sure the source water tap is turned on.
  2. Check the water pressure — if another tap is also slow, the household supply is the problem.
  3. Straighten the inlet hose and remove any kinking.
  4. Clean the mesh filters of the water hose (rinse out grit and debris).
  5. Restart the cycle. Note: when 4E shows, the washer first drains for about 3 minutes, during which the Power button does not respond — wait for that to finish.

When a reset may help

A power cycle clears a one-off glitch, but a closed tap, kinked hose, or dirty filter will bring 4E straight back. Fix the supply first.

When to call a professional

  • 4E persists with the tap open, good pressure, and clean filters
  • The inlet valve leaks or makes no sound when it should be filling
  • You are not comfortable disconnecting hoses
Safety warning: Unplug and turn the taps off before disconnecting any hose. Dry up spills before restoring power. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 4E and 4C?

They are the same fault. Samsung renamed the code on newer models: older machines show 4E, newer ones show 4C. The checks are identical.

Why does the Power button not respond when 4E shows?

On this fault the washer first drains for about 3 minutes as a safety step. Wait for the drain to finish; the controls respond again after that.
