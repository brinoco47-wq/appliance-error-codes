LG LE — Locked motor error — the motor is overloaded

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerLG
Error codeLE
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The motor is drawing too much current, usually because the drum is overloaded or something is stopping it from turning freely. The machine stops to protect the motor.

Common possible causes

  • An overloaded drum
  • A foreign object caught between the drum and the tub
  • A motor or sensor fault (less common)

Safe initial checks

  • How full is the drum? It should never be packed tight
  • Turn the drum slowly by hand (machine off) and feel for grinding or catching
  • Look for small items trapped at the door seal

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Switch off and unplug the machine.
  2. Remove some of the laundry so the drum is no more than about three-quarters full.
  3. Check around the door seal and, if you can, between drum and tub for trapped objects.
  4. Let the motor cool for about 30 minutes.
  5. Plug in and restart with the smaller load.

When a reset may help

LE often clears after the motor cools and the load is reduced. If it returns immediately with a normal load, do not keep restarting — the motor or its sensor needs checking.

When to call a professional

  • LE returns with normal, everyday loads
  • The drum will not turn by hand, or grinds
  • There is a burning smell
  • The code appears even with an empty drum
Safety warning: Unplug before reaching into the drum or checking for trapped objects. Never bypass a motor protection trip — it is there to prevent overheating. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Is LE the same on every brand?

No. On LG washers LE means a locked or overloaded motor. On some other brands the same letters can mean something different — always check the code for your brand and model.

How long should I wait before retrying?

About 30 minutes, to let the motor cool. Restarting immediately after an overload trip can trip it again.
