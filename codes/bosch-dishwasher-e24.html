Bosch E24 — Drain error — the dishwasher is not draining

ApplianceDishwashers
ManufacturerBosch
Error codeE24
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The dishwasher is not draining correctly. Water stays in the bottom of the tub at the end of the cycle, or the machine stops mid-cycle because the drain circuit is not clearing water fast enough.

Common possible causes

  • A clogged or kinked drain hose
  • A missing or sagging high loop under the sink
  • A blocked garbage disposal connection (including a knock-out plug never removed on a new disposal)
  • A dirty sump filter
  • A drain pump problem (see also E25)

Safe initial checks

  • Look at the drain hose under the sink for kinks
  • Check the high loop — the hose should rise up under the sink before going down to the drain
  • Check the garbage disposal connection for a blockage
  • Remove and inspect the sump filter in the bottom of the tub

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Switch off and unplug the dishwasher.
  2. Check the drain hose for kinks and confirm the high loop is in place.
  3. If connected to a garbage disposal, check that connection is clear (and, on a new disposal, that the knock-out plug was removed).
  4. Remove the sump filter from the bottom of the tub, rinse it clean, and refit it.
  5. Restore power and run a short cycle to test draining.

When a reset may help

A power cycle clears a one-off glitch, but a blocked hose or filter will bring E24 straight back.

When to call a professional

  • E24 persists with a clear hose, high loop, and clean filter
  • The drain pump makes no sound or hums without pumping
  • Water backs up into the sink
Safety warning: Unplug before reaching into the sump or touching the drain pump area. Keep the water supply off if you disconnect any hose. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between E24 and E25?

Both are drain faults. E24 points to the drain circuit generally (hose, filter, connection). E25 points more specifically at the drain pump being blocked or jammed.

Can a new garbage disposal cause E24?

Yes — a common one. If the disposal's dishwasher knock-out plug was never removed, the dishwasher cannot drain into it at all.
