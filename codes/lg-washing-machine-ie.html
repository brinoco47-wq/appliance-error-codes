LG IE — Water inlet error — the washer is not filling

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerLG
Error codeIE
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The washer is not receiving enough water within the time it expects. The cycle usually stops before washing properly begins, because there is too little water in the drum to wash with.

Common possible causes

  • A water supply tap that is closed or only partly open
  • A kinked, squashed or blocked inlet hose
  • A clogged mesh filter where the hose meets the inlet valve
  • Low household water pressure
  • A frozen inlet hose in cold weather
  • A faulty water inlet valve (less common)

Safe initial checks

  • Confirm both hot and cold taps are fully open
  • Look along both inlet hoses for kinks or crushing
  • Check whether another tap in the house runs normally, to judge pressure
  • Look at the mesh filters inside the inlet valve connections for grit or debris

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Switch the washer off and unplug it, and turn both supply taps off before you touch any hose.
  2. Turn both taps fully on again and check the pressure at a nearby tap.
  3. Straighten any kinked inlet hose and make sure it is not squashed behind the machine.
  4. Disconnect each inlet hose (keep a towel ready), remove the small mesh filter at the valve end, rinse it clean with an old toothbrush, and refit it.
  5. Reconnect the hoses firmly, turn the taps on, plug in, and run a short cycle.
  6. If the code returns with clean filters and good pressure, the inlet valve itself is the likely fault.

When a reset may help

A power cycle (unplug for a minute, then plug back in) clears a one-off glitch, but it does not fix a blocked filter or a closed tap. If IE returns straight away, the underlying cause is still present.

When to call a professional

  • The code persists after filters are clean and pressure is normal
  • Water is leaking at the inlet valve or hose connection
  • The valve hums or buzzes but no water enters
  • You are not comfortable disconnecting hoses
Safety warning: Unplug the machine and turn the taps off before disconnecting any hose. Water and electricity do not mix — mop up spills before restoring power. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Is IE always a serious fault?

Usually not. In most cases it is a closed tap, a kinked hose, or a dirty inlet filter — all of which you can check in a few minutes.

Can low water pressure cause IE?

Yes. If the house pressure is very low, the washer may time out before it fills. Check another tap: if that is also slow, the problem is in the household supply, not the washer.
