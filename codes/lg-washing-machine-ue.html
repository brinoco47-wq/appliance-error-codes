LG UE — Unbalanced load — the drum cannot spin evenly

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerLG
Error codeUE Also shown as: uE.
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The laundry inside the drum is unevenly distributed, so the machine cannot spin safely. A lowercase uE means the washer is still trying to rebalance the load on its own; an uppercase UE means it has given up and needs your help.

Common possible causes

  • One heavy item (a blanket, bathrobe, or pair of jeans) on its own
  • Heavy and light items mixed together
  • Too small a load to spread around the drum
  • A machine that is not level, so loads always settle to one side

Safe initial checks

  • Pause the machine and look at how the load is sitting
  • Check whether the machine rocks when you push its corners
  • Note whether the code appears with one particular item or with every load

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Pause the cycle and wait for the door to unlock.
  2. Redistribute the laundry evenly around the drum — untangle sheets and spread heavy items out.
  3. For a single heavy item, add two or three towels to balance it.
  4. Close the door and restart the spin.
  5. If the machine rocks, adjust its feet until it stands firm and level, then retry.

When a reset may help

No reset is needed for UE. It is not a fault — it is the machine protecting itself from a violent spin. Fix the load and the code clears.

When to call a professional

  • UE appears with normal, balanced, everyday loads
  • The drum bangs loudly even when the load is even
  • The machine has moved across the floor or its feet are damaged
Safety warning: Never open the door mid-spin. Wait for the drum to stop completely before reaching inside. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between uE and UE?

Lowercase uE means the washer is still attempting to rebalance the load itself. Uppercase UE means it could not rebalance and stopped for you to fix the load.

Why does one blanket always cause UE?

A single heavy item cannot spread around the drum, so one side is always heavier. Adding a couple of towels gives the drum something to balance against.
