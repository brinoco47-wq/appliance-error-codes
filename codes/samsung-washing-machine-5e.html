Samsung 5E — Drain error — the washer is not draining

ApplianceWashing Machines
ManufacturerSamsung
Error code5E Also shown as: 5C.
VerificationMeaning cross-checked against multiple published sources. Confirm in your exact model's manual — codes vary by model.

What this code means

The washer cannot drain water from the drum. Newer Samsung models show the same fault as 5C. The cycle stops with water left in the drum.

Common possible causes

  • A blocked debris filter
  • A drain hose installed incorrectly, kinked, or blocked
  • A clogged water hose mesh filter (listed in Samsung's own checks for this code)
  • A drain pump fault

Safe initial checks

  • Look at the debris filter for lint, coins, and small objects
  • Check the drain hose routing, height, and any kinks
  • Listen for the drain pump running during the drain step

Step-by-step troubleshooting

  1. Switch off and unplug the machine, with towels and a tray ready.
  2. Clean the debris filter thoroughly.
  3. Make sure the drainage hose is installed correctly — no kinks, no sagging loops, and the end positioned as the manual requires.
  4. Clean the water hose mesh filter as well.
  5. Refit everything firmly and run a drain or spin cycle.

When a reset may help

A power cycle clears a one-off glitch only. If 5E returns, the filter, hose, or pump still needs attention.

When to call a professional

  • 5E persists with a clean filter and a correctly routed hose
  • The pump is silent or hums without draining
  • Water leaks from the pump or hose connections
Safety warning: Unplug before opening the filter. Water will spill — work slowly and dry the area before restoring power. See also what to do before troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 5E and 5C?

They are the same fault. Older Samsung machines show 5E; newer ones show 5C. The checks are identical.

Where is the debris filter?

On most Samsung front-load washers it is behind a small cover at the bottom front of the machine. Your model's manual shows the exact location.
